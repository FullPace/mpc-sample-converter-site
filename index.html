Convert MPC Sample projects for your standalone MPC.

Works with the big standalone MPCs — MPC One, MPC Live and MPC X/XL. They can already import MPC Sample projects, but they do it their own way. This app rebuilds them inside your own template, so every converted beat keeps your mixer, submixes, sends and master FX exactly the way you set them up.

Features

Your template, kept 1:1

Pick your MPC template once. The converter keeps its mixer, submixes, sends, master FX and full track layout untouched — only the samples and sequences come over from the source project.

16-pad grid with preview

See every loaded pad across all 8 banks (A–H). Click a pad's play button to preview the sample, stop it any time — handy for the long loops. FX-routed pads are marked so you always know what's wet.

Split pads into drum groups

Assign pads to one or more drum groups before converting. Name groups from a preset list (Drums, Bass, Chops, Guitar, Loops, Strings…) or add your own. Each group becomes its own MPC program.

Rename sequences & keep Song Mode

Every sequence (and the Song Mode playlist) is carried over. Rename each one from musical presets — Intro, Verse, Chorus, Bridge, Buildup, Break, Drop, Outro — or your own labels.

Manage your own name presets

Add, delete and reset the group- and sequence-name presets. Your custom names are remembered between sessions, so your workflow stays yours.

Banks as Programs

One click turns each used pad bank into its own drum group. If your workflow is already organised by banks, this saves you from assigning pads to groups by hand. Toggle Pad FX, Color Compressor and Disk/RAM streaming right from the same row.

  • Pad FX — carry the source's pad insert effect onto the drum group.
  • Color Compressor — keep the source's master compressor when present.
  • Disk / RAM streaming — load samples to RAM to avoid the "disk streaming too slow" warning.
  • Per-pad routing — wet/dry routing is preserved exactly.
  • Automation & notes — full sequence data transfers, not just notes.
  • 4 languages — English, German, Spanish, French.

How it works

  1. 1
    Set your output folder & template. Both are remembered for next time.
  2. 2
    Drag & drop an MPC Sample project (.xpj) onto the window.
  3. 3
    Assign pads to groups and rename sequences — or just hit Start.
  4. 4
    Click START. A ready-to-open MPC project is written to your output folder. Originals are never touched.
